You will need to do a flood risk assessment if your development is any of the following:
bigger than 1 hectare (ha)
in an area with critical drainage problems as notified by the Environment Agency
a change of use in development type to a more vulnerable category (for example from commercial to residential) and in an area that could be affected by sources of flooding other than rivers and the sea (such as surface water or reservoirs)
If your development does not fall into any of these categories, you only need to download the flood map for planning on this page showing your flood zone to include in your planning application.
You can still use this service to request flood risk data (also known as a product 4). You should receive your data within 20 working days. However, as your site is in flood zone 1, it’s unlikely there will be any data available.
You should also check the risk from surface water and other sources of flooding when doing a flood risk assessment.
